About Me

Ciao ciao mi chiamo Pietro Gorini e sono nato a Faenza, in Italia, al volgere del secolo.
I’m an artist working across various media, such as painting, installation, sculpture, and sound.
An alumnus of the Accademia di Belle Arti di Bologna (IT), Northumbria University (UK), Camberwell College of Arts (UK), and Central Saint Martins (UK), I use art as a dynamic interface through which I establishe a dialogue between myself, the audience, and reality, and how identities, myths, and belief systems are constructed through stories, symbols, and acts of substitution and sublimation.
Perennemente inspirato dal regno naturale e dalla zoologia, folklore, personal history, and political narratives, I create works in which animals, objects, and fictional entities become proxies for human experiences.
Moving between the oneiric and the material through fable-esque dynamics, I inquisitively explore themes of inheritance, transformation, trauma, and self-determination, inviting viewers to challenge their own boundaries and ordeals.
Così facendo, la mia arte assume le caratteristiche d’un mezzo di resurrezione; an attempt to reconstruct identity in the wake of absence and complexity.
This site is a collection of my projects and visual thoughts, albeit scattered.

Manifesto

I’m looking for a miracle.

Roars of thunder and flashes of light scorch the earth in search of fertile soil; I'm drooling. I wonder what flavour is yet to come I hope it’s chocolaty even though I say I don’t like it that much.

Lines are forming between the dots, like ancient constellations you point your index at, and I'll pretend to know about them all already.

I’m looking for a miracle; pining for it to materialise before me in any form, shape, wave, or noise. The sound of awe is the coming of Heaven on Earth, with no limitations. It was written on a Jehovah’s Witness pamphlet so it ought to be true they wouldn’t lie to me.

I want it.

People around me always seemed to possess pieces of a puzzle which I did not even know were there, as though they exhumed a completeness I could never fathom. These dark impressions left me feeling somewhat defective, gasping for air whilst in stasis at the bottom of a vast, murky body of water.

From there, godless, isolated, stupid, and crooked, I create art to process and interact with reality, its inhabitants and myself, repeating those ancient human acts of storytelling to make sense of the world.

This ritual allows me to share private, intense sides of myself which would otherwise remain in a pupal state. Without it, I would engage with my surroundings through reflection, acknowledging the past only as a refraction. Art allows me to create the ground on which I walk, embodying my present as I move through the air instead of phasing through it. Sometimes, I think I can catch glimpses of the future.

Such a powerful form of communication puts me in a delicate spot, though, and to guard myself, I encode my memories, thoughts, emotions, impressions and desires in the language of fairy tales, fables, myths and dreams. By alchemically channelling these stories through my undying love for the zoological realm, psychology, and philosophy, I explore paradoxes of identity, power dynamics, trauma, self-determination, and lyrical predestination.

I'm theorising hypotheses; designing cosmogonies.

In this process, art becomes both a tool of resurrection, as an attempt to reconstruct identity in the wake of trauma and complexity, and as a forged instrument of political enquiry.

I’m looking for a miracle.

I was promised a miracle, and I waited for it to appear before me. But I waited a long time; paused in an unnatural stasis, an erosive stillness. As I awaited, it began to dawn on me that perhaps the miracle would not come. Perhaps, I will have to trigger a miracle. Maybe I will have to design my miracle, and there’s a chance I might have to become my own miracle.

Because the sound of awe might have come unnoticed. Perhaps the sky has already fallen, or maybe the rupture has occurred without me, leaving me in these ruins. Here, in this perpetual state of aftermath, all I can do is move forward, wandering uncharted ways and feeling it all, looking up at the night sky for cosmic guidance, where I find it: all products of my own design.

Thank you for being here.
